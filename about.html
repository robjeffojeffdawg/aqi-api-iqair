Overview
What is this?

Jeffo Blogs' AQI Monitor is a real-time air quality tracking application that helps you understand the air you breathe, wherever you are in the world.

Using live sensor data from IQAir's global monitoring network, it gives you instant AQI readings, health advisories, historical trends, and outdoor safety guidance — all in one place.

Understanding the data
What is AQI?

The Air Quality Index (AQI) is a standardised indicator of air pollution levels. It tells you how clean or polluted your air is, and what associated health effects might be a concern. Higher numbers mean greater risk.

0 – 50 Good Safe for everyone
51 – 100 Moderate Acceptable for most
101 – 150 Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups Limit outdoor activity
151 – 200 Unhealthy Everyone affected
201 – 300 Very Unhealthy Health alert
301+ Hazardous Emergency conditions
Under the hood
How it works

The app uses your device's location to find the nearest air quality monitoring stations and displays real-time pollution data including PM2.5, PM10, ozone, NO₂, and other pollutants.

Data is provided by IQAir, one of the world's leading air quality platforms. Historical readings are stored in MongoDB and used to build trend charts over 24 hours, 7 days, and 30 days.

City pages are server-side rendered so that Google can index them — making it easier for people searching for air quality in specific cities to find the data they need.

Privacy
Your data

Your location is only used to fetch nearby air quality data. No personal information is stored or transmitted to any third parties.

Location access is requested only when you click "Use My Location" and is never saved to a database. Favourite cities are stored locally in your browser only using localStorage.
