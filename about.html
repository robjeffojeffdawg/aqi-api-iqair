About
Real-time air quality tracking, worldwide.
What is this?

Jeffo Blogs' AQI Monitor gives you instant air quality readings, health advisories, and historical trends for any location in the world. Live data is pulled from three independent sources and shown in one clean interface.

Data Sources

IQAir — professional monitoring stations worldwide.

PurpleAir — community-operated sensors providing hyper-local PM2.5 readings.

OpenAQ v3 — UK DEFRA and European government networks, particularly useful for UK/Europe coverage.

Privacy

Your location is used only to fetch nearby air quality data. Nothing is stored server-side. Location is requested only when you click "My Location" and is never saved to a database.
