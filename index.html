About This Project

What is it?

Real-time air quality monitoring powered by IQAir, PurpleAir, and OpenAQ. Instant AQI readings, health advisories, and historical trends — worldwide.

Data sources

IQAir for global professional stations, PurpleAir for community sensors, and OpenAQ for UK/Europe government monitoring (DEFRA). Historical data stored in MongoDB Atlas.

Privacy

Your location is only used to fetch nearby data. Nothing is stored server-side. Favourites use localStorage only.
